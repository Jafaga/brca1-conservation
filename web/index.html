How to read these results

Score adds +2 for a matching letter, −1 for a mismatch, and −2 per gap letter. Matching letters is percent identity within the local alignment. Coverage tells us how much of the input piece the alignment used.

Compare scores, identity, and coverage across these species for the selected gene. The small pieces come from one gene, so a broader study still needs full sequences and more orthologs.
